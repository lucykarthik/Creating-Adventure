Chapter I

The First Steps

It all began with a simple classroom arrangement. 📚 I got to know you through a friend of my group leader. Back then, I never imagined that such a small connection would lead to a friendship that would mean so much to me. 🫶

Chapter II

The Shared Chaos

From surviving exam pressure and endless record writing to all the fun we had in class, our school days were filled with so many little moments. 📚 And those bus rides back home, talking about how the day went, followed by our messages even after reaching home… somehow, there was always something more to share. 😂💬 Looking back, it's amazing how those ordinary everyday moments became some of my favourite memories. 🥹✨

Chapter III

The Quiet Constant

As school days turned into different routines, life slowly became busier for both of us. We may not talk as often as we used to, but our messages and calls still keep our friendship going 💬. I know we don't have to talk every day to remain best friends, but honestly, I still miss having you around in person. Those face-to-face conversations, random laughs, and little everyday moments are things that messages and calls can never quite replace. Even with life taking us through different routines, you'll always be someone whose presence I genuinely miss ✨.

Chapter IV

The Open Horizon

There's still so much waiting for us beyond the school days we've left behind. Different goals, new experiences, and plenty of things life has yet to bring our way. 🌷 I hope that, even as we grow older and follow our own paths, we still find little moments to meet, talk, laugh, and create new memories together. I genuinely wish to see you achieve everything you've been working towards and find happiness in every chapter ahead. And who knows? Maybe years from now, we'll look back at all these little memories and laugh about how it all began. Here's to everything still waiting for us! ✨
